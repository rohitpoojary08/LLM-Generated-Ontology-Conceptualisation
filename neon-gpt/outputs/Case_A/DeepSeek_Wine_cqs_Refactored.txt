**Competency Questions for the Core Wine Ontology Module**

**I. Wine Identification & Classification**
1.  What is the unique identifier (URI) for a specific wine?
2.  What is the name/label of a given wine?
3.  What type of wine is it (e.g., Red, White, Sparkling, Fortified, Rosé, Dessert)?
4.  Is this wine classified as a still or sparkling wine?
5.  What is the sweetness level of this wine (e.g., dry, off-dry, semi-sweet, sweet)?
6.  Is this wine a varietal wine (primarily made from one grape variety)?
7.  Is this wine a blend? If so, which grape varieties are blended?
8.  Does this wine have a protected designation of origin (PDO) or geographical indication (GI)?
9.  What is the legal classification/appellation of this wine (e.g., AOC, DOCG, AVA)?
10. What is the alcohol content (ABV) of this wine?
11. What is the residual sugar content of this wine?

**II. Production & Provenance**
12. Which winery/producer made this wine?
13. In which wine region was this wine produced?
14. What is the country of origin for this wine?
15. What is the specific sub-region or vineyard site for this wine?
16. What is the vintage (harvest year) of this wine?
17. What were the general climatic conditions for this wine's vintage in its region?
18. What grape varieties were used to produce this wine?
19. What is the genetic lineage/parentage of the grape varieties used?
20. Which viticultural practices (e.g., organic, biodynamic, sustainable) were employed for the grapes used in this wine?
21. What specific vineyard management techniques (e.g., pruning, canopy management) were used?
22. What was the yield per hectare for the grapes used in this wine?

**III. Winemaking Process**
23. What are the key winemaking processes involved in producing this wine?
24. Was this wine fermented using wild/native yeasts or cultured yeasts?
25. What type of fermentation vessel was used (e.g., stainless steel tank, concrete egg, oak vat)?
26. Did this wine undergo malolactic fermentation?
27. Was this wine aged? If so, for how long?
28. What type of vessel was used for aging (e.g., new French oak barrel, used American oak barrel, neutral oak, stainless steel)?
29. Was the wine aged on its lees?
30. What clarification/fining methods were used (e.g., bentonite, egg whites, none/unfined)?
31. What filtration method was used before bottling?
32. How was this wine bottled (e.g., under cork, screwcap, other closure)?

**IV. Sensory & Chemical Profile**
33. What is the color of this wine (e.g., ruby red, garnet, pale lemon, deep gold)?
34. What is the intensity of the wine's aroma?
35. What are the primary aroma families detected (e.g., fruity, floral, spicy, vegetal, oak-derived)?
36. What specific aroma/flavor descriptors apply (e.g., blackcurrant, violet, vanilla, tobacco)?
37. What is the perceived body/weight of the wine (light, medium, full)?
38. What is the acidity level (low, medium, high)?
39. What is the tannin level (low, medium, high, for reds)?
40. What is the alcohol perception (light, warming, hot)?
41. What is the finish length (short, medium, long)?
42. What is the overall quality rating or score of the wine (if applicable)?
43. What are the main chemical analysis results (pH, total acidity, volatile acidity, etc.)?

**V. Relationships & Context**
44. Which other wines are produced by the same winery?
45. Which other wines are produced in the same region?
46. Which other wines are made from the same grape variety?
47. Which other wines are of the same vintage?
48. Which other wines are of the same type/classification?
49. Who is the winemaker responsible for this wine?
50. What is the history or founding date of the producing winery?
51. What is the production volume of this wine?
52. What is the recommended drinking window (peak maturity) for this wine?
53. At what temperature should this wine be served?
54. What type of glassware is recommended for this wine?

**VI. Food & Pairing**
55. What foods does this wine pair well with?
56. What types of cuisine does this wine complement (e.g., French, Italian, Asian)?
57. What specific dishes are recommended pairings for this wine?
58. What cheese varieties pair well with this wine?
59. Is this wine suitable as an aperitif?
60. Is this wine suitable for pairing with dessert?

**VII. Regulatory & Commercial**
61. What are the labeling requirements fulfilled by this wine?
62. What import/export regulations apply to this wine?
63. What is the price range or market value of this wine?
64. Through which distributors or retailers is this wine available?
65. What are the sustainability or ethical certifications held by this wine/producer (e.g., vegan, Fair Trade)?

**VIII. Advanced & Inferential Queries**
66. List all red wines from a specific region with high tannin levels and suitable for long-term aging.
67. Find all white wines from a given vintage that underwent oak aging and pair with shellfish.
68. Show all sparkling wines produced using the traditional method (Méthode Champenoise).
69. For a given grape variety, list all wine regions where it is successfully cultivated.
70. Find wines that are similar to Wine X based on shared grape variety, region, and winemaking style.
71. Which wines are blends containing a specific percentage of a particular grape variety?
72. List all wines produced using biodynamic viticultural practices in a certain country.
73. Identify wines that received a quality rating above a specific score in a given vintage year.
74. Find wines that are suitable for a specific dietary restriction (e.g., vegan, low-sulfite).
75. For a given food item (e.g., grilled steak), recommend wines based on pairing guidelines, region, and style.
76. What is the typical flavor profile evolution (aging potential) for wines of this type from this region?
77. Based on modeled terroir characteristics (soil, climate), which grape varieties are most suitable for a new vineyard in a specified location?
