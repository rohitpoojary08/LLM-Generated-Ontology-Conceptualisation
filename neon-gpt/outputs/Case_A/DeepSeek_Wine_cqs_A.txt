**Competency Questions for the Core Wine Ontology Module**

**A. Wine Classification & Basic Properties**
1.  What is the color classification (red, white, rosé) of a given wine?
2.  Is a given wine sparkling or still?
3.  Is a given wine fortified?
4.  What is the sweetness level (dry, off-dry, sweet) of a given wine?
5.  What is the alcohol by volume (ABV) percentage of a given wine?
6.  What is the residual sugar content of a given wine?
7.  What is the pH level or titratable acidity of a given wine?

**B. Composition & Origin**
8.  Which single grape variety is a given monovarietal wine made from?
9.  What are the grape varieties used in a given blended wine, and in what proportions?
10. What is the genetic lineage or parentage of a given grape variety?
11. In which specific wine region (e.g., Napa Valley) is a given wine produced?
12. Under which appellation or geographical indication (e.g., AOC Bordeaux, DOCG Barolo) is a given wine classified?
13. What are the sub-regions within a given major wine region?
14. Which country is a given wine region located in?
15. What is the specific terroir (soil type, mesoclimate) associated with a given vineyard site?

**C. Production & Provenance**
16. Which winery produced a given wine?
17. When was the producing winery founded?
18. What viticultural practices (e.g., organic, biodynamic, trellising system) were used in growing the grapes for a given wine?
19. What specific winemaking processes (e.g., carbonic maceration, barrel fermentation, lees aging) were used to produce a given wine?
20. What type of vessel (e.g., French oak barrel, stainless steel tank, concrete egg) was used for aging a given wine, and for how long?
21. Was malolactic fermentation applied to a given wine?
22. What filtration or clarification method was used before bottling?

**D. Vintage & Temporal Context**
23. What is the vintage year (harvest year) of a given wine?
24. What were the general climatic conditions (e.g., hot/dry, cool/wet) for a given vintage in a specific region?
25. How is the overall quality of a given vintage year rated for a specific region?
26. Which wines share the same vintage year?

**E. Sensory Profile & Evaluation**
27. What are the primary aroma and flavor descriptors (e.g., blackberry, vanilla, tobacco) noted in a tasting note for a given wine?
28. What is the perceived body (light, medium, full) of a given wine?
29. What is the perceived level of tannins (low, medium, high) in a given red wine?
30. What is the perceived level of acidity (low, medium, high) in a given wine?
31. What is the finish length (short, medium, long) of a given wine?
32. What is the overall quality score or rating (e.g., Parker points) assigned to a given wine?
33. Who authored a specific tasting note or review?

**F. Food & Contextual Pairing**
34. With which specific food dishes or categories (e.g., grilled steak, roasted chicken, hard cheese) is a given wine recommended to pair?
35. What is the rationale for a specific food and wine pairing (e.g., "complements richness", "contrasts saltiness")?
36. Which wines are recommended to pair with a given food item?
37. In which cultural cuisine context is a specific wine pairing traditionally recommended?

**G. Regulatory & Commercial Aspects**
38. Which regulatory standards (e.g., AOC, DOCG, AVA rules) does a given wine comply with?
39. What are the specific labeling requirements for a wine from a given appellation?
40. What is the permitted grape variety list for a given geographical indication?
41. What is the maximum allowable yield for a given appellation?
42. What is the production volume of a given winery?
43. What is the signature style or flagship wine of a given producer?

**H. Relationships & Inferences**
44. List all wines produced by a specific winery.
45. List all wines made primarily from a specific grape variety.
46. List all wines originating from a specific wine region or appellation.
47. List all red wines from a specific region that have high tannin levels.
48. List all white wines that underwent oak aging.
49. List all sparkling wines produced using the traditional method (Méthode Champenoise).
50. List all wines from a given vintage year that are rated above a certain score.
51. Which wines share similar sensory profiles (based on tasting note attributes)?
52. For a given grape variety, which regions is it most commonly grown in?
53. Which winemaking processes are typically used for a given wine style (e.g., Orange Wine)?
54. What are the typical viticultural challenges for a grape variety in a specific region?
