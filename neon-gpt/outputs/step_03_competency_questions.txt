**Competency Questions for the Wine Ontology Core Module**

**Category 1: Wine Identification and Classification**
1.  What is the specific type of this wine (e.g., Red, White, Sparkling, Fortified)?
2.  What is the color of this wine?
3.  Is this wine classified under a protected designation of origin (PDO) or other appellation?
4.  What is the alcohol content of this wine?
5.  Is this wine considered light-bodied or full-bodied (for red wines)?

**Category 2: Grape Varieties and Lineage**
6.  Which grape variety or varieties is this wine made from?
7.  What are the parent varieties (genetic lineage) of a specific grape cultivar?
8.  Is a given grape variety a *Vitis vinifera* or a hybrid?
9.  Which wines are made primarily from the Cabernet Sauvignon grape?
10. What are the typical flavor profiles associated with a specific grape variety?

**Category 3: Production and Origin**
11. In which wine region was this wine produced?
12. Is the production region considered an Old World or New World region?
13. Which winery produced this wine?
14. Is the producing winery commercial, boutique, or a cooperative?
15. What viticultural practices (e.g., Organic Farming, specific Pruning Methods) did the winery employ for this wine?
16. What winemaking processes (e.g., Fermentation, Aging) were used in its production?
17. Was the wine aged in oak barrels or stainless steel tanks?

**Category 4: Vintage and Climate**
18. What is the vintage (harvest year) of this wine?
19. How would you characterize this vintage (e.g., Excellent, Good, Poor)?
20. What were the key climate conditions (temperature, rainfall) that influenced this specific vintage?

**Category 5: Sensory Characteristics and Composition**
21. What are the tasting notes (Aroma, Flavor, Mouthfeel) for this wine?
22. What chemical compounds (e.g., Tartaric Acid, Tannins, Anthocyanins) are present in this wine?
23. Which compounds contribute to the wine's acidity?
24. Which compounds act as preservatives (e.g., Sulfur Dioxide) in this wine?
25. Does this wine contain specific additives?

**Category 6: Food and Pairing**
26. What are the recommended food pairings for this wine?
27. Is this wine particularly recommended for pairing with cheese, meat, or dessert?
28. Which wines are recommended to pair with a specific food (e.g., steak, goat cheese)?

**Category 7: Regulatory and Commercial Aspects**
29. What regulatory aspects (labeling requirements, production standards) is this wine subject to?
30. Does the wine label comply with specific regulations?
31. Under which commercial product classification (UNSPSC code) is this wine sold?

**Category 8: Relationships and Inferences**
32. List all wines produced in the Bordeaux region.
33. Find all wineries that use organic farming practices.
34. Identify all full-bodied red wines from excellent vintages.
35. Show all sparkling wines that have tasting notes containing "citrus" aromas.
36. Which wines have an alcohol content greater than 14%?
37. Find wines that are derived from a specific ancestral grape variety.
38. List wines that are suitable for a given set of food pairing categories.
